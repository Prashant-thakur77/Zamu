# Zamu: taste profile

(Derived from the project; assumptions noted at the end.)

## Personality
IS
- **Grounded**: heavy, confident type; solid fills, not glass. Money feels like it has weight.
- **Communal**: the circle of names is always the hero; people's names before numbers, never wallet addresses.
- **Exact**: shillings to the shilling, receipt codes in mono, ticks that mean "delivered", not "sent".

NEVER
- **Crypto-casino**: no neon gradients, glowing coins, charts, "to the moon" energy.
- **Bank-corporate**: no navy-and-grey dashboards, stock photos, tiny grey legalese.
- **Charity-poster**: no pity imagery, no "empowering the unbanked" tone.

## Audience taste
M-Pesa, WhatsApp, Safaricom app: big amounts, green for money in, very plain language, SMS-style confirmations. Trust comes from receipt codes they can check and from seeing who paid. Swahili/English code-switching is normal ("Zamu yako", "Umelipa", "Asante"). Green reads as money received; red as debt, used sparingly.

## Brand anchors
- Base repo logo and indigo palette: **replace** (identity of another product).
- New mark: a ring of seats with one lit seat (the turn), drawn as a kanga-like circle of dots. Wordmark "zamu" lowercase, heavy.
- Colours: deep "chai" ink, warm paper, M-Pesa-adjacent leaf green for delivered money only, a sun/turmeric yellow for "your turn", clay red for overdue.

## Feel in motion
Heavy and precise, like a rubber stamp: quick in (180-260 ms) with a firm settle (custom ease out, slight overshoot only on the "Paid" stamp). The turn moving around the ring is slow and deliberate (900-1200 ms). SMS receipts slide up like a phone notification (320 ms). No floaty parallax.

## Material and texture
Matte paper with a faint grain; printed-ink borders like a kanga hem; ruled lines like a treasurer's book; mono receipt text. Flat solid colour blocks, hard edges, small radii (6-10 px), hairline rules.

## Taste anchors
- M-Pesa confirmation SMS: the most trusted UI in Kenya; borrow its format for receipts.
- Kanga cloth: a printed border plus a proverb in the "jina" panel; borrow the border band and the proverb line.
- Treasurer's exercise book: ruled rows, names, ticks; borrow for the member list.
- Matatu sign-writing: bold, warm, hand-made confidence in display type.

## Lines not to cross
- No wallet addresses as primary labels (names first, addresses tucked away).
- Green only means money delivered. Never use green for "submitted".
- No celebratory confetti on someone else's debt; overdue states are calm and factual.
- No animation that delays seeing the amount or the status.

## Assumptions
Prashant was not asked taste questions (he asked for no interruptions). The profile is derived from the product, the Kenyan context and the competition (judged inside Nimiq Pay on a phone).
