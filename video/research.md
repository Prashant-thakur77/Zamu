## Facts used
- 8.7 million Kenyan adults use informal groups (chamas, ROSCAs, ASCAs), 2024 FinAccess Household Survey (CBK,
  KNBS, FSD Kenya): https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/2024-FINACCESS-HOUSEHOLD-SURVEY-MAIN-REPORT.pdf
- Treasurer risk: groups "operate entirely on trust" and a treasurer "quietly mismanages group money" (K24, 2026);
  a Mombasa chama treasurer moved Sh163,000 to "her personal mobile money account" (Bizna Kenya, 2022). Used only as
  the general framing "the whole group has to trust them", no specific claim on screen.
- Live payout quote: video/film/public/facts.json (video/facts.ts, at render time).

## What this film borrows
- Real product within the first seconds (cold open on a real receipt landing).
- One plain pain line, then the turn.
- Shown inside a phone, labelled honestly (local test with mock wallet and partner; live site for home and demo).
- Burned-in key phrases; end card with name and how to open it in Nimiq Pay.
